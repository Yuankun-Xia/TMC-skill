# TMC context and source boundaries

## Fit the contribution to the system problem

TMC covers mobile-computing architectures, services, algorithms/protocols, environments, communication systems and applications. Its official scope guidance connects ML and lower-layer work to a clear mobile-computing contribution and evaluation. Explain the relevant operational benefit, cost, assumptions and boundary.

Do not equate TMC with FL alone or require physical mobility in every paper. Relevant constraints may be limited resources, intermittent connectivity, real-time behavior, on-device operation, security or a mobile application.

## Local evidence versus journal rules

This bundle was prepared from 36 user-provided PDFs, totaling 568 PDF pages, on 2026-10-25. Their topics emphasize FL, edge learning, resource heterogeneity, scheduling, privacy, vehicular/UAV and space networks. Metadata/structure were inventoried for all files; selected relevant pages and figures were examined for writing/evaluation examples. This is not a full correctness review of every paper or a representative sample of all TMC.

Reusable choices include linking design to system constraints, defining actual resource costs, separating simulation from on-device evidence, and matching claims to assumptions and budgets. A practice in a published paper is not a journal requirement. Do not copy its numerical settings, wording or claimed guarantees as defaults.

- [IEEE Computer Society author resources](https://www.computer.org/publications/author-resources). Use the current journal-specific submission link for exact stage/article-type requirements. This bundle deliberately fixes no page limit, fee, abstract word count, anonymity rule or supplement policy.
- [IEEE research reproducibility](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/create-your-ieee-journal-article/research-reproducibility/). Supports detailed methodology and encourages data/code sharing; it does not establish a universal TMC public-release mandate.

If exact submission rules or tool/API behavior may have changed, verify the relevant primary source at use time. Report inaccessible pages instead of importing an old rule from another IEEE journal. Use current author/editor instructions when they apply.
